About the Team

The Knights Baseball Program is an East Bay, CA‑based 501(c)(3) focused on player development, baseball IQ, character, and love for the game.

Knights 12U Valdez is made up of 13 hardworking and dedicated boys, four passionate and patient coaches, and all of their supportive (and busy!) families. Many of the boys have played together since their 8U season, and our newer families have only added to the camaraderie and strength of the team.

We practice and compete year‑round in tournaments at the highest levels in California and around the West. Our players train hard, play with heart, and represent their families and communities with pride - on and off the field.

About Cooperstown 2027

We are currently preparing for one of youth baseball’s most iconic experiences: a trip to Cooperstown All Star Village in June 2027. Over 1,300 12U teams and their families - 100 per week - make the summertime trek to the Cooperstown, NY area to compete, immerse themselves in baseball history and culture, and most importantly, create forever memories.

Players and coaches stay in cabins onsite - no parents allowed! - and play 7+ games throughout the week, while fans cheer from the stands. They’ll also take part in the opening ceremonies and parade, compete in skills competitions, and visit the National Baseball Hall of Fame as a team, to honor the ballers who came before.

All funds raised go directly to covering the team’s entry fees and tournament costs - no player left behind.
